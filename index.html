Political Language: NLP & RAG

PROJECT 02 · MACHINE LEARNING

Predicting Disaster Economic Impact

Undergraduate thesis investigating disaster-related economic damage prediction with EM-DAT data. Compared regression models and missing-data strategies, while assessing dataset bias, interpretability, and ethical deployment.

PROJECT 03 · EXPERIMENTATION

SwapSlot: Causal Business Analytics

Designed and analyzed a randomized survey experiment for a last-minute booking concept. The final analytical sample included 134 respondents; a discount treatment increased perceived attractiveness (β ≈ 0.66, reported p ≈ 0.02).

RRCTCausal InferenceBusiness Analytics
Team project · Repository being prepared
PROJECT 04 · COMPUTATIONAL MODELING

Andes Virus: Network Epidemic Simulation

Modeled a hypothetical outbreak in a synthetic community of 10,000 individuals using deterministic epidemic models and stochastic contact networks. Explored how isolation, contact tracing, and quarantine change simulated outbreak risk.
